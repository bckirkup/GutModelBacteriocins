## Independent read of the headline numbers

The recomputed per-arm medians confirm the published conclusion:
realized lysis per producer division is amplitude-invariant (5 % arm:
0.045 / 0.046 / 0.044 across amp 0/15/60) while kills-per-lysis and
delta slope scale with retardation amplitude — the Stage D dose
response carries a transport component, not only the lysis prior.
The amp-60 (shipped pI-derived retardation) and amp-0 (literature
D_eff) endpoints bracket amp-15 (deployed Stage D value) at every
target, monotonically.

The committed 33-run retardation analysis reproduces exactly from the

## Corrections — 2026-10-21 (Edison review, independently re-verified here)

Edison Scientific's review of PR #449 flagged endpoint/metadata wording.
Each correction below was re-verified against the local raw objects
before recording.

- **All 24 status sidecars carry a stale `sim_time_s = 20000`** regardless
  of true termination — do not read run endpoints from sidecars. True
  `run_provenance/termination_time` values: endpoint runs 4 × 17,400 s,
  5 × 18,000 s, 3 × 19,500 s, 6 × 20,400 s; mid runs 3 × 18,000 s,
  3 × 20,400 s (totals 4/8/3/9). The original wording "ended
  16800–20400 s" conflated the shared comparison endpoint with true
  termination: 16,800 s is the t_common floor, not a run endpoint; the
  shortest true termination is 17,400 s.
- **Saved-agent snapshots lag true termination.** Three endpoint runs'
  last `agents/step_*` is step_000320 (19,200 s) vs terminal 19,500 s;
  the mid/endpoint split above uses `termination_time`, not the last
  saved snapshot.
- **"First attempt" is unverified**, not corroborated: the sidecars carry
  no attempt-count fields and per-child AWS Batch attempt records were
  not exported by the collector surface. `spot_interruption = false`
  and `resume_from_checkpoint = false` are consistent with
  first-attempt completion but cannot establish it.
- **This is a replay, not an independent estimator**: the reproduction
  used the committed `analyze.py` unmodified. Anchor input bytes were
  hash-matched to the manifest but not byte-compared against the
  earlier independently audited copies.
- **kills_per_lysis is not monotone in amplitude.** At the 5 % target it
  declines: medians ≈ 8.0 (amp 0) → 3.3 (amp 15) → 2.1 (amp 60). It
  rises at 0.25 % and 1 % targets. The monotone-across-amplitude claim
  applies to the slope and tail-median responses (ordered in all 9/9
  target×seed combinations), not to kills per lysis.
- Realized-lysis similarity across amps at the 5 % target (0.045 /
  0.046 / 0.044, three screening seeds) is descriptive comparability,
  not formal equivalence/invariance. Cumulative ratios at each run's
  own end, fixed-calendar contrasts, and all-strains vs target-strain
  deaths are distinct definitions and were kept distinct.
