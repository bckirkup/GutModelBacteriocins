# Layer-3 gradient readout — independent audit (2026-10-20)

Scope: the six committed run series under `analysis/spec13_gradient/`
({gradient, uniform} × seeds 1001–1003). Method: re-ran
`run_readout.py --analyze-only` over the committed `out/*_timeseries.csv`
and `out/*_prov.json`, compared every field of the recomputed
`readout_summary.json` against the committed copy, then checked the
report's qualitative claims directly against the raw series. No
simulations rerun; this audits committed CSV/JSON, matching the evidence
class of the source files.

## Reproduction — PASS

Every scalar in `readout_summary.json` reproduces; the 37 diffs are all
in the 15th–17th significant digit (e.g. occupancy `…862` vs `…8626`),
i.e. float print noise, not drift. `verdicts.csv` regenerates
identically. The committed analysis pipeline is honest about the
committed raw rows.

## Run integrity — PASS

- All six prov records: `termination.cause = 0` ("reached configured
  horizon"), `steps_completed = 4320` = 72 h at dt = 60 s.
- `ledger.closed = true` on all six arms; `stocks == expected` exactly in
  each ledger (gradient 4.50056e9, uniform 3.12496e9). The `ledger_ok`
  column in the timeseries is consistent with this.
- Prov config echoes match `run_readout.py`'s constants: transit
  12/8/10 h, pH 6.25/6.60/6.80, purge at 172800 s = 48 h, fraction 0.9,
  `n_patches_per_region = 400`, flora proxy 1e7 CFU/mL,
  `uniform_profile` false/true on the correct arms.

## Gate verdicts — verified

- Stool CFU/g: 8.28e6 (gradient) / 8.12e6 (uniform) over the post-purge
  24 h tail — inside the 1e6–1e8 band. (The prov `stool_cfu_per_g`
  observable reports 1.4e7 over a different window; also in band.)
- Flatness: 2.07–2.96 gradient, 1.15–1.38 uniform — under the declared
  ≤4.0 bound.
- Proximal→distal fraction spread is real: cecum 0.135–0.138,
  transverse 0.19–0.21, descending 0.28–0.41 in the gradient arm;
  collapses to ~0.16–0.21 in uniform. The gradient contributes the
  spread, as claimed.

## Occupancy saturation — VERIFIED, with wording corrections

The report's qualitative claim stands: occupancy saturates to ~1.0 by
24 h in **both** arms, confirming that the Layer-2 0.1–1 % "stationary"
band is transient once the lumen grows and `p_attach = p0·(1−occ)`
feedback drives occ → 1. The measured trajectory is steeper than the
report's prose suggests:

- 1 % crossed at ~0.2 h (first summary row, all arms) — not "at ~6 h".
- 10 % crossed at 3.3–4.7 h.
- At 12 h: gradient 0.74–0.91 across regions; uniform 0.50–0.81 — the
  uniform arm's cecum is still ~50–60 %, so "~78–84 % by 12 h in both
  arms" overstates the uniform arm.
- At 24 h: 0.975–1.0 everywhere. Saturated.

The report's mechanism attribution (lumen growth sustains reseeding;
standalone Layer 2's pool drains at the 4 h half-life) is consistent
with the ledger: luminal births ≈ 1.8e10 (gradient) / 1.2e10 (uniform)
dwarf mucosal births ≈ 4–5e5 by ~10⁴×.

## Fraction level vs flatness — confirmed as flagged

Distal fractions 0.28–0.41 (cecum 0.135) against the 1e7 CFU/mL flora
proxy are ~10–40 % — above the empirical ~1–3 % Enterobacteriaceae
fraction. The report correctly presents this as a parameter consequence
(`patch_supply_scale`/`flora_density_cfu_ml`), not a fitted quantity.
This is the live scientific tension: the same parameters that produce
in-band stool CFU/g produce supra-empirical mucosal fraction. The
flatness gate passing does not resolve the level question.

## Seed robustness — verified

Stool CFU/g agrees across seeds to <0.1 %; flatness ranges overlap.
Population-scale observables are effectively deterministic; per-lineage
composition is the stochastic part (lumen_lineages differ per seed).

## What this audit does not cover

- Layer-2's 4,374-arm sweep raw execution evidence — `sweep_configs/` /
  `sweep_runs/` are absent from this tree, so the Phase-1 admissible
  corner is taken as reported, not raw-verified.
- Per-lineage/genotype stool composition (committed series carry
  `lumen_lineages` counts but no BI-genotype breakdown).
- Runtime identity: prov files record seed/dt/parameters but no
  `git_sha`, so the producing binary version is not pinned by the
  committed evidence.

## Verdict

The committed Layer-3 analysis is internally consistent and reproducible:
gates and the headline coupling finding (luminal feedback saturates
mucosal occupancy, overturning the Layer-2 stationary band) are
supported by the raw series. Two wording corrections to carry forward:
occupancy crosses the band in the first hour, not "~6 h", and the 12 h
figure is ~50–91 % across regions/arms rather than a flat 78–84 %. The
scientifically open item is the fraction **level** (0.14–0.41 vs ~0.01–0.03
empirical), which the flatness gate does not constrain — it belongs on
the parameter-decision list, not the defect list.

## Corrections — 2026-10-21 (Edison review, independently re-verified here)

Edison Scientific's review of PR #448 independently recomputed the
series and corrected several of this audit's replacement numbers, which
were themselves imprecise. Re-verified against the committed CSVs:

- **Occupancy at exactly 12 h**: gradient 0.715–0.8725, uniform
  0.48–0.82 across regions (this audit said 0.74–0.91 / 0.50–0.81;
  the original report's "~78–84 % by 12 h" was wrong in both arms).
- **Occupancy at 24 h**: gradient 1.0 in all regions; uniform
  0.9675–0.9975 (this audit's "0.975–1.0 everywhere" overstated the
  uniform cecum at 0.9675).
- **The "1 % crossing" is ambiguous**: initial patches are
  Bernoulli-seeded at an expected 1 %, so the first sampled occupancy
  above 1 % is not an unambiguous growth-driven crossing. First strict
  exceedances across all six series: 0.167–0.667 h.
- **The nominal post-purge tail spans 47h50–71h50**, not 48–72 h: the
  CSVs' last row is t = 258,600 s (total_time 259,200 s configured; the
  final ~10 min is unsampled). A 48–72 h sensitivity preserves the
  stool verdict.
- **Retention is not established.** The report's "effective ~29 h
  in-chain" is not computed by the supplied analyzer, and the scored
  verdicts file checks stool/flatness only. Resolved configured transit
  sums are 30 h (gradient) and 24 h (uniform); configured transit is
  not a measured microbial retention time.
- **Seed agreement is not determinism**: stool CFU/g is nearly identical
  across seeds (<0.1 %), but flatness relative spans are ~37 %
  (gradient) and ~18 % (uniform) across the three seeds; region
  composition is the stochastic part.
- Provenance files record seed/dt/parameters but no producing binary
  `git_sha` — noted above and carried forward.

The fraction-level parameter question recorded here was resolved
2026-10-21 by PR #450 (denominator re-anchored to the sourced FISH
scale 1e8 cells/mL; same runs report 1.4–4.1 %).
