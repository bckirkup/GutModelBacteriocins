### Empirical mapping summary

This synthesis reconciles an initial 210-DOI Consensus API literature survey ([consensus_doi_index.csv](consensus_doi_index.csv)) with primary full-text, table, and figure verification across all 41 quantitative benchmarks ([consensus_empirical_benchmarks.csv](consensus_empirical_benchmarks.csv)). A central methodological rule governs every recommendation below: **bulk chemical extracts, whole-tissue or intraluminal sensors, leading-edge transit markers, and steady-state community phage ratios are empirical observables—not direct measurements of GutIBM's microscopic C++ state variables.** Where a mapping requires unmeasured partitioning or allocation assumptions, the resulting interval is explicitly classified as a **Cycle 2 sensitivity hypothesis / model prior**, rather than a hard empirical pass/fail gate.

1. **Bulk vs. Unbound Colonic Vitamin B12 (`GAP-01`, `b12_initial_conc`):** Shipped `1.0e-3 mol/m^3` (`1 uM`) numerically matches **hot-extracted bulk** human fecal corrinoids (`9.66e-4 mol/m^3` in Allen & Stabler 2008 Table 4, where `>98%` are non-cobalamin analogues and directly tabulated bulk cobalamin is `19 ng/g = 1.40e-5 mol/m^3`, `71.3x` lower). In C57BL/6 mice (Kelly et al. 2019 Results & Fig. 1e, correcting an internal `ng/ml` typo in that paper's abstract), bulk active cobalamin is `0.08 ng/mg` cecal (`5.90e-5 mol/m^3`, `16.9x` below `1.0e-3`) and `0.06 ng/mg` fecal (`4.43e-5 mol/m^3`, `22.6x` below `1.0e-3`), rising under oral supplementation (`3.94 ug/mL` water) to `10.60 ng/mg` cecal (`7.82e-3 mol/m^3`) and `7.81 ng/mg` fecal (`5.76e-3 mol/m^3 = 5.76 uM`, `5.76x` above `1.0e-3`). None of Allen, Kelly, Forgie, or Kundra directly measures **unbound extracellular receptor-accessible ligand** or dominant `[2-MeAde]Cba` affinity for `BtuB`. Consequently, `1–15 nM` (`1.0e-6 to 1.5e-5 mol/m^3`) is an **exploratory free-ligand sensitivity prior**, and resolving Spec 14 V1 requires decoupling `BtuB` competitive shielding (`1 + sum_i C_i,free / K_i`) from growth-limiting corrinoid cofactor supply.
2. **Mucosal Oxygen Compartments & Anaerobic Maintenance (`GAP-08`, `chem_env.oxygen.*`):** Relative to the `42 mmHg ~ 5.5e-2 mol/m^3` (`55 uM`) tissue annotation in `src/io/chem_environment_config.h:25`, shipped `epithelial_conc = 55.0e-6 mol/m^3` (`0.055 uM = 0.042 mmHg`) is an exact `1,000x` `uM -> mM` unit conversion slip (inactive by default and forced off in Layer-2 live slots). Biologically, however, `~40 mmHg` (`~5.24e-2 mol/m^3`, Albenberg et al. 2014) measures **vascularized serosal/mucosal tissue** (a Robin reservoir), whereas mouse cecal lumen is censored at `<1 mmHg` (`<1.31e-3 mol/m^3`); a Dirichlet apical mucus boundary is therefore host- and compartment-dependent rather than universally `42 mmHg`. Similarly, `anaerobic_maintenance_factor = 15.0` is not uncited—`docs/PARAMETERS.md:528-541` derives it from `~30` aerobic vs `~2` fermentative `ATP/glucose` anchored to Pirt (`0.22`) and Varma & Palsson (`0.25 mmol Glc/gDW/h`, `NGAM = 7.6 mmol ATP/gDW/h`)—and is inactive while `carbon_maintenance_rate = 0.0`. Because *E. coli* respiratory chains operate at `P/O = 1.0–1.5 ATP per O atom` (`~14–20 ATP/glucose`, Carlson et al. 2026), `3.4–8.0x` serves as a **conditional stoichiometric sensitivity range** when non-growth carbon maintenance is enabled.
3. **First-Order Luminal Washout `tau` vs. GI Transit Landmarks (Pillar 3, `layer3.transit_h`):** In `src/layer3/lumen.cpp`, `transit_h` is a **first-order luminal washout e-folding time** (`half-life = ln(2) * transit_h`, coupled to reattachment and substrate relaxation), not geometric plug-flow travel time or first-stool dye appearance. The shipped `12 / 8 / 10 h` (`30.0 h` sum) is a defensible **human-oriented reference scenario** (`3.8%` above the `15.9–28.9 h` range of human capsule study central estimates in Nandhra et al. 2023 and `2.21x` the `13.6 h` cohort median in Procházková et al. 2024), while `14–20 h` (human fast-turnover) and `~4.0 h` (`1.0 / 1.5 / 1.5 h` murine fast-turnover) are **provisional sensitivity scenarios** rather than direct empirical inversions of carmine or SPECT arrival landmarks.
4. **Colicin Ecology Scale & Temperate Prophage Priors (Pillar 4, `GAP-09`, `GAP-10`):** Primary full-text audit corrects three key ecological benchmarks: (i) Bakkeren et al. (2025) tested engineered *E. coli* + `4%` sorbitol **in vitro** (no mouse experiments) to illustrate their rare-invader theorem; (ii) Kirkup & Riley (2004) tracked **cage-level strain replacement** across 3 co-housed mice (`1%` pellet CFU detection limit, single pellets rarely mixed), making it a metapopulation/censoring benchmark (`GAP-09`) rather than within-patch mucosal coexistence; and (iii) Henrot et al. (2026 preprint) studied an **E7/E9-related endonuclease (`pEndo`)** (not canonical ColE2) and found **no significant *in vivo* difference in phage titres or lysogenization** vs bacteriocin-deletion controls over 10 days in dixenic mice. Finally, Lopez et al. (2025, `10.1016/j.cub.2025.03.073`, PMC13255779) infer `0.001–0.01 events/bacterium/day` (`1.16e-8 to 1.16e-7 /s` via `/ 86400`) as a **whole-community human contextual prior**, which should not be applied as a hard veto against elevated monoxenic mouse *E. coli* lambda hazards (`1.70e-6 to 4.44e-6 /s` in De Paepe et al. 2016).

## 2. GAP-01: Colonic Vitamin B12, Bulk Extraction vs. Free Ligand, and Corrinoid Speciation

**Unit harmonization & extraction caveat:** Cyanocobalamin `MW = 1355.37 g/mol` at assumed wet density `rho = 1.0 g/mL` gives `1 ng/g ~ 1 ng/mL = 7.378e-7 mol/m^3` (`0.7378 nM`) and `1 ug/g (1 ng/mg) = 7.378e-4 mol/m^3` (`0.7378 uM`). Crucially, hot KCN/buffer homogenate assays (`B12-01..B12-04`) liberate intracellular and protein-bound corrinoids into bulk total pools; under competitive `BtuB` equilibrium, apparent toxin dissociation follows `Kd_app = Kd_tox * (1 + sum_i C_i,free / K_i)`, where both native unbound `C_i,free` and non-cobalamin cobamide affinities `K_i` remain unmeasured in gut mucus.

**Reconciled interpretation:**
- **Kelly et al. (2019) unit correction (`B12-03`):** While the published abstract misprints fecal B12 in `ng/ml`, the Results text and Figure 1e report mass-normalized `ng/mg` for both cecal (`0.08 -> 10.60 ng/mg`) and fecal (`0.06 -> 7.81 ng/mg`) homogenates. Thus baseline mouse bulk fecal cobalamin is `44.3 nM` (`4.43e-5 mol/m^3`, `22.6x` below `1.0e-3`), and high-dose water supplementation reaches `5.76 uM` (`5.76e-3 mol/m^3`).
- **Allen & Stabler (2008) & Hallberg et al. (2022) (`B12-01`, `B12-02`, `B12-06`):** Allen's Table 4 directly tabulates `19 ng/g` cobalamin (`1.40e-5 mol/m^3`, `1.4%` of `1309 ng/g`) and `5.5%` `[2-MeS-Ade]Cba` (correcting an abstract typo of `15.5%` that made percentages sum to `110%`). Hallberg's human profile (`8` corrinoids = `7` complete cobamides + cobinamide; Simpson sum-of-squares `0.41`) reuses Allen's `n = 18` dataset rather than adding a second human cohort.
- **Kundra et al. (2022) medium & batch partitioning (`B12-05`):** The `5 ng/mL` (`3.69e-6 mol/m^3`) MacFarlane figure is added recipe CNCbl, whereas EIA measured `8.9 +/- 0.1 ng/mL` (`6.57e-6 mol/m^3`) starting `T0` B12 with vitamin mix and `4.1 +/- 0.4 ng/mL` (`3.03e-6 mol/m^3`) without added B12 (`NB12`, expectedly below the `30 ng/mL` UHPLC-DAD LOD). At `T24`, intracellular B12 exceeded supernatant B12 in `5/8` donors (`D3, D5–D8`) while `3/8` showed the opposite, and supernatant immunoassay totals do not prove unbound monomeric state.
- **Modeling guidance:** Treat `1.0e-6 to 1.5e-5 mol/m^3` (`1–15 nM`) strictly as an **exploratory free-ligand sensitivity scenario** alongside explicit sweeps of effective analogue `kd_corrinoid_btuB` (`docs/RECEPTOR_LIGAND_PARAMETERIZATION.md:52-55`), while noting that lowering a single shared `[B12]` field simultaneously alters growth (`km_b12 = 1e-6`) and `MetE` fitness costs across C, S, and R strains.

### 3.2 Stoichiometric & Chemostat Maintenance Context (`chem_env.oxygen.anaerobic_maintenance_factor`)

In `docs/PARAMETERS.md:528-541`, `anaerobic_maintenance_factor = 15.0` is documented as a coarse stoichiometric ratio (`~30 ATP/glucose` aerobic divided by `~2 ATP/glucose` fermentative at equal ATP maintenance, anchored to Pirt `0.22` and Varma & Palsson `0.25 mmol Glc/gDW/h`, `NGAM = 7.6 mmol ATP/gDW/h`). It is **inactive by default** (`carbon_maintenance_rate = 0.0`, `metabolic_switch_enabled = false`) and distinct from growth-associated `anaerobic_carbon_cost_factor = 4.1`. Under matched substrate and biomass conventions, `F = (m_ATP,anaer / m_ATP,aer) * (eta_ATP,aer / eta_ATP,anaer)`.

**Reconciled interpretation:**
- **Oxygen boundary selection:** Correcting the `55.0e-6` unit annotation mismatch does not mean every simulation should hard-code a `42 mmHg` (`5.5e-2 mol/m^3`) Dirichlet boundary at `z = 0`. In Albenberg et al. - **Maintenance & `yield_carbon` conventions:** None of `MAINT-01..MAINT-06` provides a single-strain paired zero-growth carbon-maintenance ratio across an `O2` switch.
